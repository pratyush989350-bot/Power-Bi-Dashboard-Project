# Power-Bi-Dashboard-Project
Blinkit Performance Analysis Presentation (2025-26)
Slide 1: Executive Summary
Business Performance Dashboard Snapshot
Total Revenue: ₹903.40K (Gross Sales after discounts)

Total Volume: 5K (Total orders successfully placed on the platform)

Average Order Value (AOV): ₹180.68 per transaction

Average Delivery Time: 13.47 Minutes

Customer Base: 3K Total Registered Customers (1K Repeat Customers)

Slide 2: Major Operational Bottlenecks
Critical Areas Hindering Business Growth
1. Alarmingly Low Order Success Rate:

Data Insight: The current Success Rate % is at 0.59 (59%). According to the status breakdown, 20.48% of orders are Refunded and 20.04% are Cancelled.

Business Impact: Out of every 100 orders, 41 are failing to complete. This represents a massive leakage in revenue, high operational costs for reverse logistics, and severe damage to customer trust.

2. High SLA Breach Rate (Delivery Delays):

Data Insight: The Late Delivery % stands at 24%.

Business Impact: Nearly 1 in every 4 orders takes longer than 15 minutes. In a hyper-competitive quick-commerce market, delivery delays prompt instant customer churn to competitors like Zepto and Instamart.

3. Low Customer Retention:

Data Insight: Out of 3K total customers, only 1K fall under the Repeat Customers segment.

Business Impact: While user acquisition is healthy, poor operational fulfillment (cancellations and delays) prevents customers from turning into loyal, habitual buyers.

Slide 3: Revenue & Demand Opportunities
Positive Data Trends to Leverage
1. High-Volume Grocery Categories:

Data Insight: The product category treemap shows that Dairy, Bread & Eggs and Household Essentials contribute the largest share to total revenue.

Strategic Advantage: These are daily-use essential items that keep users engaged with the app on a regular basis.

2. Strong Logistics Monetization:

Data Insight: The business generated ₹75K strictly from Total Delivery Revenue.

Strategic Advantage: This proves that customers are highly receptive to convenience fees during peak hours, bad weather, or high-demand slots, opening a steady side-revenue stream.

Slide 4: Strategic Action Plan
Recommendations for Operations & Management
Recommendation 1: Implement Real-Time Inventory Syncing:

To fix the 41% cancellation and refund loop, the tech team must integrate automated real-time inventory tracking between the consumer-facing app and the Dark Stores. Products must show as "Out of Stock" instantly if unavailable on the shelves.

Recommendation 2: Dynamic Rider Allocation During Peak Hours:

Address the 24% late delivery rate by deploying dynamic rider surge-payouts in slow zones. Filter the dashboard by Dark_Store_ID to target the worst-performing warehouses and optimize their local delivery radius.

Recommendation 3: Push Cross-Selling & Product Bundling:

With an AOV of ₹180, profitability is thin. Leverage the high traffic of the Dairy, Bread & Eggs category by triggering smart cart recommendations for high-margin products like Snacks & Munchies or Personal Care just before checkout to lift the average basket value past ₹250.

Recommendation 4: Targeted Win-Back Campaigns:

Re-engage the 2K non-repeat customer base using targeted push notifications and exclusive loyalty coupons tied directly to an "on-time delivery guarantee."
